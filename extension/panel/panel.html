When you open a doc, repo, Notion page or ticket, deja-view checks what you already have open for something older that relates. At most one nudge an hour and three a day. Titles and URLs are checked locally; a page’s content is only looked at on a page that triggers.

deja-view reads the tabs you already have open, and turns what you have already read into a skill your coding agent can run later.
