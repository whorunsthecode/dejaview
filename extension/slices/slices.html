Slices

A themed piece of your reading, written out as one Markdown document you can send to someone. Nothing is generated until you have seen exactly what would go in it.

Themes in your open tabs

Grouped by what they have in common, or by the name you gave the tab group. Tabs that fall into no theme are left out rather than swept into a pile called “other”.

Saved slices

Kept on this machine. Reopen one to edit it, or regenerate it once you have read more on the theme — anything you removed stays removed.

What would go in

Left out because of where they came from, not what they say. This is a default, not a judgement, and the list will not catch everything — check the list above as well. Switch any of them back on to include it.
